Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Your files

FixHub opens and cleans your spreadsheet inside your own web browser. Your file and its contents are not uploaded, stored or shared by us. When you close or reload the page, the data is gone.

Accounts

There is no sign-up and no login. We do not collect your name or email address through the tool.

Technical data and third parties

This site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. The cleaning tools load two open-source code libraries from public content delivery networks (cdnjs and jsDelivr), which may log requests as well. The print formatter loads its libraries from this website and processes Excel and CSV files on your device.

Cookies and ads

The tool itself does not use cookies. If we show advertising or use analytics in the future (for example Google AdSense or Google Search Console), those providers may use cookies or similar technologies, and we will update this page before doing so.
