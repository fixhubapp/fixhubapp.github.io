What FixHub checks

Most spreadsheets that come from forms, exports and copy-paste have the same small problems. FixHub finds them all and shows you exactly where each one is.

  • Duplicate rows, shown together so you can keep one and delete the rest
  • Extra spaces at the start, end or middle of a cell
  • Phone numbers written in different formats, or with a missing + and country code
  • Invalid phone numbers and invalid email addresses
  • Empty rows and empty cells in rows that have data

How to use it

  1. Choose your .xlsx or .csv file, or drop it on the page.
  2. Look at the fault cards. Each one shows how many problems it found.
  3. Click a card. The tool jumps to the first problem and highlights it. Click again to see the next one.
  4. Press the fix button for one problem, or "Fix all" for the whole sheet.
  5. Download the cleaned file. Your original file is never changed.

Your file stays on your device

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on how many files you check.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is opened and cleaned inside your own browser. It is never sent to a server.

Will my formatting and formulas stay the same?

The cleaner skips formula cells and keeps rows in place in workbooks with formulas. Colors, borders, charts and images are not kept in the downloaded file. When pasting back into your original file, paste only into non-formula cells to retain formulas.

Which files can I use?

Excel files (.xlsx) and CSV files (.csv). If your file has several sheets, you can switch between them and clean each one.
